How to Play:

• Click empty space near the star to add a planet — its orbit is placed automatically by its ratio, and you pick its sound & pitch

• Double-click near an orbit ring to edit that planet's pitch & sound
• Hold (~2s) on an orbit ring to delete that planet
• Press Enter to name & save the system
• Max 7 planets per star
